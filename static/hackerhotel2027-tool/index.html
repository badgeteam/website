Badge not in the list?

Some USB‑C cables only charge. Try another cable.

Firefox and Safari can't connect to USB devices. Use Chrome, Edge or Opera.

Linux: give your user access to the badge (1209:d643) and to its boot ROM (1a86:55e0) with a udev rule, for example in /etc/udev/rules.d/99-hh27-starfish.rules:

SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTRS{idVendor}=="1a86", ATTRS{idProduct}=="55e0", MODE="0666"
SUBSYSTEM=="usb", ATTRS{idVendor}=="1209", ATTRS{idProduct}=="d643", MODE="0666"

Then run sudo udevadm control --reload and plug the badge in again.

Windows: the badge needs no driver. A badge with older firmware may not show up: install the WinUSB driver for it once with Zadig: Options > List All Devices, pick the badge (USB ID 1209 D643), driver WinUSB, Install Driver. Then update its firmware.

    Badge: connect it to see its firmware.

    Help: drivers, browsers and recovery

    The page restarts the badge into its USB boot ROM and asks you to pick it: "Unknown device from QinHeng Electronics". No badge connected? On the badge, hold A for the menu, press C to reach UPDATE, and press A. On newer firmware, holding C and D together for 3 seconds also works, even when the menu does not: an arrow runs down the LEDs, then the boot ROM starts. Your items, name and settings stay.

    Linux: the udev rule under "Badge not in the list?" covers the boot ROM (1a86:55e0) too.

    Windows: once, install the WinUSB driver for the boot ROM with Zadig: Device > Create New Device, USB ID 1A86 55E0, driver WinUSB, Install Driver. Without it the boot ROM never shows up in the list.

    Firefox and Safari can't connect to USB devices. Use Chrome, Edge or Opera, or flash with minichlink -w ledmatrix.bin flash -b.

    If an update stops half way, the badge starts in its boot ROM: update again. If it no longer starts at all, unplug it (and its battery) and plug it in again, or bring it to the badge recovery area at the event.

    Create an animation

    Draw on the starfish frame by frame, or start from a demo, a video or a text. Then save it to the badge.

    255

    The badge shows what you draw and play here. Starting an item or pressing Stop playing (under On your badge) ends this until you draw or press Play again.

    Click or drag to paint. Round dots are white LEDs; diamonds are colour LEDs. With the keyboard: Tab to the board, arrows move, Space paints, Enter on a diamond sets its colour.
